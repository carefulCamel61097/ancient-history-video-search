Why alternative ancient history?

Some of it is nonsense. Not all of it.

Each line below is a claim. Open it for the argument. Open the argument for the evidence — photographs, figures, and the video where someone makes the case.

This page is scaffolding. The structure is real and the mechanism works; every word of argument on it is placeholder text, marked in quoted italics. The arguments get written by hand out of the knowledge base, which is being built first for exactly that reason.
