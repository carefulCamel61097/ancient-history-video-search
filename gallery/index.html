The arguments these serve

The same photographable thing supports different arguments, and each wants a different selection of images. Taken from plan/TOPICS.md.

tagthe argumentwhat the images have to carryexamples
cross-continentalThe same distinctive detail appears in places with no known contact.Location for every image, ideally coordinates. Visual similarity obvious side by side.nubs at Sacsayhuamán, Menkaure, Yangshan, the Propylaea · polygonal walls in Peru and Japan · pillowy faces in Peru and Egypt · the handbag symbol at Göbekli Tepe, in Sumeria, in Mesoamerica · flood myth
two-industriesAt one site there are two different qualities of work that are not the same craft, and the difference is not acknowledged.Both qualities in one frame wherever possible. Close enough to see surface and tooling.Serapeum boxes: machined faces, crude hieroglyphs cut into them · Giza casing precision beside later patching · fine megalithic base under coarser work at Ollantaytambo
out-of-sequenceThe oldest work at a site is better than what was built on top of it.Both layers in one frame, or a clear before/after pair.Ollantaytambo · Gunung Padang · laterite base under brick in Thailand
whyNobody has a good explanation for why they did this.The feature, clearly, in context. Location matters less.nubs that hold nothing · the Osireion's water · scoop marks · the polish inside Barabar
howThis is hard to explain with the toolkit they are said to have had.Scale, precision, tool marks, hardness of material. Something for scale.Serapeum box tolerances · Egyptian stone vases · Giza granite machining · mortarless polygonal joints
scaleMoving and placing masses we would find awkward today.The object with a person or vehicle beside it; the route from the quarry.Baalbek trilithon · Yangshan monolith · Osaka Castle megaliths · Ollantaytambo ramp
datingThe physical evidence sits badly with the accepted date.Weathering, erosion, stratigraphy, excavation photographs.Sphinx enclosure erosion · Göbekli Tepe · disputed radiocarbon and luminescence results at Giza
unfinishedAbandoned work shows the method, because it was caught mid-process.Half-done objects and live quarries.Unfinished Obelisk at Aswan · Menkaure's casing · Yangshan · half-screeded blocks
convergenceSeveral independent lines point the same way.Usually a composite: map, chart, and photographs together.Younger Dryas: spherules, Carolina Bays, meltwater pulse, myth · casting: knobs, scrape marks, slump, indents
deflatedAn alternative claim examined and found wanting, by someone on the alternative side.The evidence that dissolves it, usually mundane and usually a photograph of an ordinary object.the Jiroft "handbags" are solid stone with no opening · the Assyrian genie's bag as a pollen bucket for date palms
mainstreamWhat the accepted explanation is, and exactly where it stops being convincing.The standard account's own evidence, shown fairly.lifting bosses as the accepted reading of knobs · copper and sand abrasion · construction ramps · Sphinx erosion as wind and sand
